Every kid deserves a big league walk up.

HitterHype turns your phone into a real PA system for baseball and softball teams. Every player on the roster gets their own walk up song and a custom announcement, just like the big leagues, without a soundboard or a press box.

Download on the App Store

Free to try for 7 days. iPhone and Apple Watch.

What you get

  • A real PA voice announces "Now batting, number 7, Alex" before every at bat
  • Any walk up song from Files or Apple Music, trimmed to a perfect ten second drop
  • Phonetic spelling so tricky names get read right the first time
  • Two pro quality voices. A different one for each of your teams
  • Music dips when the announcer talks, then returns to full volume

Game day ready

  • Add your roster in seconds. Import a screenshot or type players in
  • Drag and drop lineup so the next batter is always on top
  • One tap plays the announcement and the song
  • Pad Mode. Every player is a button, perfect for between innings hype
  • Apple Watch companion. Control playback from anywhere on the field
  • Lock Screen controls. See the current song and pause from anywhere
  • Cached announcer audio plays offline so a weak field signal doesn't kill game day

Built for any level

Youth league, high school, college, adult rec, corporate softball, tournament PA. If your team wants stadium quality walk up music without stadium quality gear, HitterHype works the same way at every level.
